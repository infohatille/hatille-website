FOR COLOUR ANALYSIS

How to prepare.

  • Daylight. Sit facing a window. Avoid warm lamps and ring lights.
  • Bare or light makeup. So your true skin tone shows.
  • Hair back. Pull hair away from your face if you can.
  • Plain top. Wear white or light grey, no prints.
  • Camera at eye level. A laptop or propped-up phone works best.
  • Quiet space. And a good Wi-Fi connection.
GOOD TO KNOW

Questions

Do prices include tax?

Yes. The price you see is the total you pay, in Canadian dollars, with all taxes included.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe, a certified payment processor. Your card details go straight to Stripe over an encrypted connection; Hatille never sees or stores them.

What do I need for the call?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a modern browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). You'll join from the link in your confirmation email.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Use the link in your confirmation email to reschedule or cancel up to 24 hours before your session for a full refund. Sessions cancelled with less notice may not be refunded.

Is the call recorded?

No. Calls are not recorded. Any photos taken during colour analysis are used only during your session to show drapes, and are not saved.
