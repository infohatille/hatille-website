About Hatille

Where fashion meets feeling good.

Hatille is a fashion, health and wellness house based in Toronto, Ontario. We believe the way you dress is one of the kindest ways to take care of yourself.

FOUNDER · HATILLE
Meet the founder

Crystel Hatille Hilton

Based in Toronto, Ontario, and originally from Dominica, the Nature Isle of the Caribbean, Crystel Hatille Hilton has studied psychology, fashion techniques and design, and is currently pursuing undergraduate studies in Digital Health Engineering Technology.

This unique blend of mind, craftsmanship, and care is at the heart of HATILLE. Named after her middle name, HATILLE is a space where fashion, wellness, and self-expression come together. Crystel believes that what we wear influences how we feel, and how we feel shapes the way we carry ourselves and show up in the world.

Her professional journey spans both healthcare and fashion, with experience in styling, merchandising, customer service, and specialty alterations. Her accessory collections have been showcased at Montreal Caribbean Fashion Week, New York Fashion Week, and the Anguilla Fashion Expo.

HATILLE was born from a desire to share the knowledge and guidance Crystel wishes she had received along her own journey. From discovering the colours that complement your skin tone and understanding how to communicate with a tailor, to caring for quality fabrics and appreciating how the simple act of getting dressed can brighten a difficult day, HATILLE is about making fashion more personal, practical, and empowering.

It is also a space where Crystel shares her creative journey, evolving interests, and passion for thoughtful craftsmanship. As the brand grows, HATILLE will introduce a collection of handcrafted bridal pieces, including delicate veils, bespoke hair accessories, and keepsake glassware adorned with hand-painted details in its signature colours of gold, black, and white.

At its heart, HATILLE is about more than what you wear. It is about how you feel, how you express yourself, and the confidence to embrace who you are.

"Thank you for being here. I can't wait to dress the journey with you."

With love, Crystel
