02 · VIRTUAL TRY-ON

See it on you.

Upload a full-length photo and we'll find your shoulders, waist and hips, then fit clothes to your body with the light of your own photo. Try a Hatille silhouette in your best colours, or a piece from any shop. Your photo never leaves your device.

How it works
  1. Add your photo. A full-length photo, standing straight and facing the camera.
  2. Choose a piece. Pick a Hatille silhouette and colour, or upload a product photo from any shop.
  3. We fit it to you. The garment snaps to your shoulders and hips and takes on your photo's lighting.
  4. Fine-tune and save. Drag to move, use the sliders to adjust size and length, then save your look.

You get 2 free try-ons. Book a consultation for unlimited try-ons with a Hatille consultant.

The photo you need
  • Full length, head to knees at least (head to toe is best)
  • Standing straight, facing the camera, arms slightly away from your body
  • Fitted, simple clothes (leggings and a fitted top are ideal)
  • Even light, like facing a window in daylight, against a plain wall
  • No side angles, sitting, crossed arms or bags over the shoulder
  • No baggy coats or big jackets (they hide your shape)
Product photos from a shop
  • Best: the garment laid flat or on a hanger, on a plain background
  • Also works: a model wearing it, facing forward
  • Avoid: busy backgrounds, white clothes on white backgrounds, side views

03 · FABRICS & CARE FACTORS

Know your fabric before you buy.

Read the care label before you shop. How a fabric wears, wrinkles and washes decides how long a piece will look new.

04 · LAUNDRY CARE

Laundry rules we live by.

Wash less, wash cold

Most clothes only need a wash after several wears. Cold water (30°C or below) protects colour and fibres and saves energy.

Inside out, zipped up

Turn darks and prints inside out, close zips and hooks, and use mesh bags for delicates and lingerie.

Skip the dryer

Heat shrinks and fades. Dry knits flat, hang shirts on wide hangers, and steam instead of ironing when you can.

Treat stains fast

Blot, never rub. Use cold water for protein stains (blood, sweat, makeup) and take silk or structured pieces to a professional.
