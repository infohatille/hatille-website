05 · FASHION THAT LIGHTENS THE DAY

Small style choices, big mood.

These little choices tell yourself that you matter, even on days you're not going anywhere.

  • 01

    Get dressed, even at home

    Swap pyjamas for something soft but intentional. It signals "the day has started".

  • 02

    Wear the special piece on a Tuesday

    Stop saving your favourite things for occasions. Joy is the occasion.

  • 03

    One joyful accessory

    A bright scarf, gold earrings or a coloured bag lifts a neutral outfit and your energy.

  • 04

    Comfort you can feel

    Cashmere, silk and brushed cotton feel calming against the skin. Choose texture on anxious days.

  • 05

    Plan tomorrow tonight

    Laying out your outfit removes one decision from a stressful morning.

  • 06

    Edit what drains you

    If something pinches, itches or makes you feel smaller, alter it, donate it or let it go.

06 · MOVE YOUR BODY

Exercise you'll actually do.

Canada's movement guidelines for adults suggest at least 150 minutes a week of moderate-to-vigorous activity, muscle strengthening at least twice a week, and breaking up long periods of sitting.

  • 01
    Walk for 20–30 minutes a day. Brisk walking counts, and it's one of the best mood boosters there is.
  • 02
    Strength twice a week: squats, push-ups, lunges or a class. Strong feels good.
  • 03
    Stand up and stretch every hour you sit.
  • 04
    Dress the part. Activewear you love makes it easier to show up.
07 · DRINK YOUR WATER

Hydration, beautifully.

A common guideline is about 9 cups (2.2 L) of fluids a day for women and 12 cups (3 L) for men, more when it's hot or you're active. Water is the best choice. Tap a glass each time you drink.

Your count is saved in this browser only and resets each day. Speak with your doctor about your own needs.

08 · DRESSING FOR SUCCESS

Dress for the day you want.

  • 01
    Fit beats price. A tailored $80 blazer looks more expensive than an ill-fitting $800 one.
  • 02
    Wear your colours near your face to look rested and confident on camera and in person.
  • 03
    Build a capsule of 10–15 pieces that mix together, so every morning is easy.
  • 04
    Mind the details: pressed fabric, clean shoes, minimal well-chosen jewellery.
  • 05
    Dress one step above the room for interviews and big meetings.
