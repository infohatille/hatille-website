I've always believed that what we wear is a conversation with ourselves before it's a conversation with anyone else. Some mornings that conversation is easy. Other mornings, nothing fits, nothing feels right, and the day starts heavier than it should.

Hatille started with a simple question: what if getting dressed could be an act of care? Not about trends or rules, but about knowing your colours, wearing clothes that truly fit, and choosing pieces that lift your mood.

## Fashion, health and wellness, together

I didn't want to build just another shop. I wanted a place where you can find your colour season, learn what to ask your tailor, understand your fabrics, and find small rituals that make you feel good, all in one house.

## What's next

Our first collection is handmade bridal pieces: veils and hair pieces, plus champagne flutes and framed keepsake glass hand-painted in gold, black and white. I'll be sharing every step here and on our socials. Thank you for being part of the beginning.
