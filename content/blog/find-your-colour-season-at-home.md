Colour analysis looks at three things: your **undertone** (warm, cool or neutral), your **depth** (light to deep) and your **clarity** (bright or soft). Together they place you in one of 12 seasons.

## 1. Use daylight

Stand facing a window, with no makeup and your hair pulled back. Artificial light is usually too yellow and skews results.

## 2. Compare, don't judge one colour

The magic is in comparison. Hold gold next to silver and a tomato red next to a blue red. Which makes your skin look smoother and your eyes brighter?

## 3. Watch for shadows

The wrong colour deepens under-eye shadows and highlights redness. The right one evens everything out, as if you've had a good night's sleep.
