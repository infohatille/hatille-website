A $60 pair of trousers, hemmed and tapered for $40, will look better than a $400 pair that pools at your ankles. Tailoring is the most underrated luxury.

## Buy for the shoulders

Shoulders are the hardest thing to alter. If they fit, almost everything else can be adjusted.

## Know what to ask for

Hems, waist take-ins, sleeve length and tapering are quick, affordable fixes that transform a piece.
