HATILLE WEBSITE: HOW TO LAUNCH IT FOR FREE
==========================================

The quickest free option is Netlify. It hosts the site and also collects
your contact form and waitlist sign-ups for you.

1. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and create a free account.
2. Unzip this folder, then drag the whole "hatille-website" folder onto the page.
3. Your site goes live within a few seconds at an address like hatille.netlify.app.
   You can rename it under Site configuration → Change site name.
4. Forms: in Netlify, open your site → Forms → Enable form detection, then
   drag the folder onto the Deploys page once more so Netlify finds the two
   forms ("contact" and "waitlist"). Then add an email notification to
   info.hatille@gmail.com under Forms → Form notifications.
5. Optional: buy a domain (for example hatille.com, about $15–25 CAD a year) and
   connect it under Domain management.

BEFORE YOU LAUNCH, UPDATE:
- Social links are all set: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube (@Hatille).
- The shop and alteration lists: open assets/data.js to add, remove or reorder
  shops. Style & Grace Cleaners is pinned at the top with "pinned: true".
- Confirm each listed shop's details and hours before promoting them.

WHAT WORKS ONCE IT'S LIVE
- Contact form and waitlist (through Netlify Forms)
- Printing the bodice block (opens your browser's print dialog)
- Everything else works now: colour draping, try-on, alteration finder,
  water tracker, blog. Photos people upload stay on their own device.
