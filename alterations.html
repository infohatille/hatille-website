04 · PATTERN BLOCK BUILDER

Your measurements, drafted.

Build a basic block (sloper) for a top, dress, skirt, pants or jacket, for women or men. Download it full size for a print shop, or as pages for your home printer, and take it to your tailor or dressmaker as the starting point for a perfect fit.

Drafted with standard block methods and checked for accuracy: matching seams agree within millimetres and every PDF prints at exact 1:1 scale with a 10 cm test square. Your first block is free; each additional block is $5. Seam allowances are not included.

Download to print

How to measure: wear fitted clothing and keep the tape snug, not tight. Nape to waist goes from the bone at the base of your neck down your spine to your natural waist. Back width runs across the back between the armpit creases, about 10–12 cm below the nape. Shoulder length runs from the side of the neck to the shoulder bone. Body rise: sit on a hard chair and measure from your waist, down your side, to the chair seat.

Every file includes a 10 cm test square. Seam allowances are not included.
